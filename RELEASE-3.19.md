# 3.19 candidate
Repeated accepted unchanged EXP-bar totals separated by more than five seconds retire the witnessed old unmatched log remainder. Newer notices remain pending. This prevents old differences from repeatedly returning after HUD outages.
The five-second reconciliation grace is a bounded policy, not a proven server limit: longer genuine HUD lag may temporarily undercount until an authoritative reading corrects it. Covered or unread notices may still be missed. Kill counts remain EXP-event estimates. Validation is recorded/synthetic, not live gameplay.
