# Class guides

Open **Tools → Class guides** and choose a first-job build. The panel includes starting stats, AP rules for equipment requirements, and cumulative SP targets through level 30. Thieves can choose a Lucky Seven or Double Stab route. The source buttons open the full level-by-level plan and the second-job guide index.

The in-app summaries are based on NiaMeowDB's [Warrior](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/guides/warrior-class-guide), [Magician](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/guides/magician-class-guide), [Bowman](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/guides/bowman-class-guide), and [Thief](https://meowdb.com/msclassic/guides/thief-class-guide) guides.
