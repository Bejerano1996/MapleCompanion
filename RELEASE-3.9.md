# Umbrale Companion 3.9

Improves identity matching for stationary partially read reward rows. A repeated amount alone no longer proves that the notice stack scrolled. Known scroll with independent surviving amounts remains supported.

Unread money rows receive two consecutive retry turns before moving to the next row. The limit remains one row and two recognition passes per poll; normal two-frame money confirmation remains required.

Validated with local images, native UI tests and synthetic timing/geometry scenarios. Unread or indistinguishable notices can still leave pickup totals incomplete. No live gameplay accuracy claim.

Close the companion before replacing program files. Preserve state.json and caches.
